How to read the map

Cyan · Catch basins: drains that collect street runoff.

Orange · Drain problems reported to 311.

In area view, brighter means more. Each area covers 1 km².

Reports / 100 drains = area reports ÷ mapped drains × 100. Reports are not linked to individual drains; this is not a failure rate.

Keep in mind

  • Reports are requests, not confirmed failures.
  • Drain counts do not show condition or capacity.
  • Rain is measured at Central Park, not across every borough.
Data preparation & downloads

  • Checked missing values, repeated IDs and locations.
  • Restored report dates by request number.
  • Kept drain IDs separate from report IDs.
  • Grouped locations into equal 1 km² areas.

Rain readings use NOAA quality checks; missing values stay missing. Several reports can concern one place. Reporting access and delays affect the record. No complainant names, contacts or street addresses are shown.
